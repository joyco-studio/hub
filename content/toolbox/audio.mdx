## What is Audio Workbench?

[Audio Workbench](https://audio.joyco.studio) is a browser-based audio tool. Drop in WAV, MP3, FLAC, OGG, AAC or M4A files to start a project, then inspect their spectrum and levels, trim with handles, normalize loudness and render out through ffmpeg.

Everything runs locally in the browser using web workers, so your audio never leaves your machine. It is powered by JOYCO's [Suno](/toolbox/suno) Web Audio library.
