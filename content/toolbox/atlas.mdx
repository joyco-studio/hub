## What is Atlas?

[Atlas](https://atlas.joyco.studio) is a web tool for preparing texture atlases. Upload a texture, define as many crop regions as you need with zoom and fit controls, then move on to arranging them into a packed layout.

It keeps a running list of every crop you mark, making it handy for game development or any workflow that needs precise texture subdivision.
