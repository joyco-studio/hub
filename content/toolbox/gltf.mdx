## What is GLTF?

[GLTF](https://gltf.joyco.studio) is a web tool for inspecting 3D model files. Drop a `.glb` or `.gltf` anywhere on the page, or load one from a URL, and browse its meshes, materials and textures right in the browser.

<Video src="https://r2.joyco.studio/hub/videos/gltf.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline width={1920} height={1080} />

You can also play back and preview any animations baked into the file. A fuzzy search (`⌘K`) lets you jump to any component by name, so you can debug or audit a model without opening a full 3D suite.
